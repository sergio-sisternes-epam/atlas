## Decision

The operational successor to okf-wiki is named **Atlas**.

## Rationale

Atlas best carries compiled, navigable, modular knowledge for skills and projects without the process baggage of the previous surface.

## Alternatives considered

Codex, tome, library, archive, and memory were evaluated; Atlas won on breadth and graph character.

## Consequences

All new operational knowledge work targets the Atlas skill and CLI contracts.
